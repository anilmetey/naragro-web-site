ABOUT US

Connecting Producers with Consumers Worldwide

"Naragro is an agricultural commodities brokerage and consultancy firm with a focus on the global markets specialized in vegetable oils and fats. Across our global network, we connect producers with consumers and bring greater transparency and trust to supply chain with our rigorous approach to risk management and compliance."

Based in Mersin, Turkey—a central maritime gateway connecting the Black Sea, Mediterranean, and Middle East—Naragro coordinates end-to-end trading execution under FOSFA and GAFTA contract terms.
